# Sources

The public documents behind [design.md](design.md) and
[rulebook.md](rulebook.md), numbered as they cite them and grouped by topic. Every entry says what
it was used for and was checked against the text on 17 October 2026 (S38
and S39 on 18 October, S40 to S48 on 19 October). Where the publisher blocks
automated access, or a document was read from a copy hosted by someone else,
the entry says so.

Nothing here comes from non-public material. The scenarios' participants are
fictional.

## Project Agorá

**[S1]** BIS Innovation Hub, *Project Agorá: A shared programmable platform
for wholesale cross-border payments*, 27 May 2026.
https://www.bis.org/publications/project-agora-shared-programmable-platform-wholesale-cross-border-payments.pdf
Page numbers are the report's printed numbers. Used for: the layered ledger
model and its status as a prototype design choice (p.24); atomic settlement
and its limits (p.2, pp.22–23, p.65); central bank control of tokenized
reserves (p.60); the legal nature of tokenized deposits (pp.59–60, p.73);
technical irreversibility versus settlement finality (p.26, pp.65–66);
confirmation of payee and the participants' signatures (p.44, p.50);
privacy (pp.43–47, pp.68–71); scope (pp.56–57).

## Market conventions

**[S29]** Department of Finance Canada, *Determining bond and treasury bill
prices and yields*.
https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/financial-sector-policy/securities/securities-technical-guide/determining-bond-treasury-bill-prices-yields.html
Used for: Government of Canada bonds paying half the annual coupon every six
months, and the actual/365 market convention.

**[S44]** International Capital Market Association (ICMA), European Repo and
Collateral Council, *Frequently asked questions on repo*, question 1, *What
is a repo?*
https://www.icmagroup.org/market-practice-and-regulatory-policy/repo-and-collateral-markets/icma-ercc-publications/frequently-asked-questions-on-repo/1-what-is-a-repo/
Used for: a repo as a sale and repurchase of securities that functions
economically as a secured loan of cash, the buyer owning the asset in the
meantime, and the repo rate as the buyer's return on the cash it lent.

**[S45]** ICMA, *Frequently asked questions on repo*, question 20, *How do
repo parties ensure they have enough collateral?*
https://www.icmagroup.org/market-practice-and-regulatory-policy/repo-and-collateral-markets/icma-ercc-publications/frequently-asked-questions-on-repo/20-how-do-repo-parties-ensure-they-have-enough-collateral/
Used for: revaluing collateral frequently, and the buyer calling variation
margin from the seller when it falls, the reverse when it rises.

**[S46]** ICMA, *Frequently asked questions on repo*, question 21, *What is a
haircut?*
https://www.icmagroup.org/market-practice-and-regulatory-policy/repo-and-collateral-markets/icma-ercc-publications/frequently-asked-questions-on-repo/21-what-is-a-haircut/
Used for: a haircut as a percentage deducted from the collateral's market
value, protecting the cash lender.

**[S47]** ICMA, *Frequently asked questions on repo*, question 22, *Who is
entitled to receive coupons, dividends or other income payments on a
security being used as collateral in a repo?*
https://www.icmagroup.org/market-practice-and-regulatory-policy/repo-and-collateral-markets/icma-ercc-publications/frequently-asked-questions-on-repo/22-who-is-entitled-to-receive-coupons-dividends-or-other-income-payments-on-a-security-being-used-as-collateral-in-a-repo/
Used for: the buyer holding legal title and receiving the issuer's income on
collateral, and paying the seller an equal amount (a manufactured payment)
under the GMRA, since the seller keeps the economic risk.

**[S48]** Bank of Canada, *Methodology for calculating the Canadian Overnight
Repo Rate Average (CORRA)*.
https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/interest-rates/corra/methodology-calculating-corra/
Used for: CORRA as the cost of overnight general collateral repo funding in
Canadian dollars against Government of Canada securities, and its compounded
index accruing over calendar days divided by 365, the actual/365 basis the
sandbox's repo interest uses.
